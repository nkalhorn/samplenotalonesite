Three partners, one through-line.

Not Alone grew out of 25 years of work with young people and families. Wise Friend makes that work widely accessible, so it can travel beyond the Summit.

The convening

Not Alone Summit

A national gathering of CEOs, artists, athletes, clinicians, researchers and youth ambassadors working together to make mental health support better for everyone.

×

The roots

Inspiring Children Foundation

The 501(c)(3) behind the Not Alone Program (Challenge, Summit and Awards), serving youth for 25 years. It’s where our methodology was built and tested.

×

The curriculum

Wise Friend

Structured, non-clinical mental and emotional fitness training for teams, schools and organizations, built from that same 25-year foundation. The tools at Not Alone, from the Life Plan to Inspire Cards to peer conversations, come from the Wise Friend curriculum.
